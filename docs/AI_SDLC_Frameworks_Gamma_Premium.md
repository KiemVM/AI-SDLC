Weaknesses: Heavy, Token intensive

## Slide 7: Spec-Kit
**Layout:** Layout: Two-column

Strengths: Discovery, Specification, Feature planning
Weaknesses: Limited governance
Best for Feature development

## Slide 8: Matt Skills
**Layout:** Layout: Two-column

Strengths: TDD, Refactoring, Architecture evolution
Weaknesses: Less product discovery
Best for Existing codebases

---
## Slide 9: Superpowers
**Layout:** Layout: Two-column

Strengths: Fast execution, Small context, Low token
Weaknesses: Weak planning
Best for Daily coding
